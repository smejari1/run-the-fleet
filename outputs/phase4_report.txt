Phase 4 complete - independent assignment safety

Built:
- fleet/safety.py independently validates every proposed assignment before
  the simulator mutates job or tractor state. No scheduling/scoring happens
  inside the validator.
- Checks tractor availability, job ownership/state/submission, implements,
  capabilities, route endpoints/roads/closures, physical travel/work estimates,
  finite fuel, work plus depot-return fuel and reserve, and field overlap.
- Late work requires an explicit at-risk flag and generates a warning.
  Beyond-horizon finishes generate warnings. Warnings and rejections differ.
- Rejected proposals are not applied; their reasons are logged and counted.
  Dispatch retries exclude the rejected pair and are bounded. Exclusions are
  cleared at the next decision, so repaired/reopened opportunities can retry.
- All three dispatchers reserve fields during active jobs (including travel).
  Arrival checks and work-history invariants provide additional protection.
- Dashboard shows independent safety status, rejected proposals and warnings;
  the comparison includes actual rejected-proposal counts.

Assumptions/limits:
- Field reservation is deliberately conservative: no other job is dispatched
  to that field until its active job ends, even if estimated work windows
  could otherwise be non-overlapping. This avoids delays creating conflicts.
- Collision protection models field work, not road-traffic spacing.
- Baseline policies can declare planned late work at risk; lateness remains
  measured as deadline misses rather than being hidden as a safety failure.
- Seeded tests cover the reported scenarios, not every possible farm state.
- Full explanations remain Phase 5. No claim of global planner optimality.
- Prior declined commit preference retained; changes remain uncommitted.
