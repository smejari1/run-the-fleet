Phase 3 complete - recovery and invariant hardening

Built:
- Recovery history records who stopped, preserved acres, remaining acres,
  who resumed, and reassignment time. Work history remains the acres ledger.
- Orphaned jobs return to pending without changing original submission age,
  priority, or deadline. All pending work is reconsidered immediately.
- No compatible non-broken tractor: job remains pending and at risk with a
  RECOVERY_WAITING entry. Repair permits recovery at the physical location.
- Shared invariant checks cover ownership, active states, complete work,
  completion lists/times, fuel bounds and non-overlapping work history.
- Invariant failures are logged; the live app pauses and requests Reset.
- Queue shows awaiting/resumed recovery status and preserved-work tooltip.
- Operator can change submitted pending-job priorities without preempting
  active work; these actions are captured in deterministic replay.
